Build OS · by Nik Jain

An operating system for shipping excellent products.

Build OS interviews me phase-by-phase while I build, writes production-grade artifacts into the repo, and grades every answer against nine craft pillars.

Then it improves its own question bank from what broke — so every project starts from a sharper bar than the last. It's a loop, not a checklist.

The loop

Build → Score → See → Improve.

Every project runs the full cycle. Skipping a step is logged, never silent.

01
Build
The OS interviews me through Discovery, Design, Build, Eval, Ship and Retro — writing 13 artifacts (PRD, ADRs, evals, failure modes, cost, safety…) into the repo as we go.
02
Score
Every answer is graded weak / good / great against a written rubric, rolled up into nine pillar scores with the arithmetic shown — never eyeballed.
03
See
The dashboard renders my current level per pillar, the trend across projects, and a question heatmap of where I keep winning or slipping.
04
Improve
Every failure mode becomes a candidate question. Show up twice, and it's promoted into the bank — the quality bar compounds.
The measure

Nine craft pillars, zero vibes.

The dimensions that separate a toy from a production system anyone would trust. Each pillar is proven by real artifacts and graded questions — a pillar with no evidence scores null, never a guess.

01
Product Judgment
Who it's for, the job it does, what "good enough" means, when to kill it.
02
System Design
Architecture, the big choices (ADRs), failure recovery, scale, versioning.
03
Evaluation
Golden datasets, validated judges, regression gates, failure-mode analysis.
04
Reliability & Ownership
Edge cases, testing, idempotency, deployed and observable.
05
Safety
Trust boundaries, guardrails, least-privilege tools, red-teaming.
06
Economics
Cost-per-use, model and caching trade-offs, the operating point.
07
Communication
Turning a vague goal into scope, and explaining it to a non-expert.
08
UX/UI & Interaction
User flows, the states users actually see, accessibility.
09
Collaboration & Stakeholders
Design, data, GTM, legal, support and eng leadership — aligned, signed off.
